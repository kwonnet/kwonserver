          ***Launch The App:***  
          Open the app, click on games, select the category, and join any game room to play.  

          ***How to Play:***  
          Random questions will be dropped each round. Try as much as possible to answer fast to earn points. The faster you answer, the more points you earn\.  
          Remember you're competing against other players around the world\.  
          Beware of the countdown timer\.  
          Your answer won't be received if the countdown timer has elapsed\.
          *Swen Ai* is your buddy to notify of the followup actions. 
          
          ***Chat & Have Fun:***
          You can chat with other players within the room and have fun while playing. Keep it civil and never be abusive as such actions will flagged your account to be suspended.
          \n Each message you type in the game room deducts coins from your available balance\. This amount is not fixed and is randomly calculated\.  

          ***Game Points***  
          Each time you answer a question, the current timer is picked automatically. At the end of the round, your points are calculated based on whether your choice is the correct answer, the timer, and the number of people in the room\. 
          \n Each time you play or participate in a game round, your coins are deducted from your available balance\. This amount is not fixed and is randomly calculated\.   

          ***Game Power:***  
          Each time you play or participate in a game round, you receive a free game power. This amount is not fixed and is randomly calculated and credited to your game power account\.  
          Even in real life, when you play, you get tired at some point—that's why we have a power gauge and turbo to boost you up\.  
          Game power keeps you energized as you play the game\.  
          Don't forget to always recharge yourself by long pressing the power gauge & turbo button to continue playing continuously\.  

          ***Game Wallet:***  
          Each user wallet has 3 balances: *bonus*, *amount*, and *credit*\.  
          - Your *bonus* balance is the amount of coins you earn from your invites, and each user receives a bonus amount to play the game upon account registration\.  
          - Your *amount* is the number of coins you buy from the game\.  
          - Your *credit* is the amount of cash you earn from the game\.  

          You can view your wallet by switching to the wallet tab\.  

          ***How To Buy Game Coins?***  
          Don't worry, we got you covered! You can easily purchase more coins under the wallet tab using Telegram stars to continue playing\.  

          ***Game Leaderboard:***  
          Each round you play, your coins are accumulated, and the ranking is updated\. You can see the leaderboard under the ranking tab\. The leaderboard is based on *Today*, *This Month*, and *The Year*\.  

          ***Game Rewards:***  
          1. At the end of the day, we reward the top 3 players with game bonuses\.  
          2. At the end of the calendar week, we reward the top 3 players with game bonuses\.  
          3. At the end of the month, a percentage of the top players will be rewarded with cash\. The top players in each category will be verified automatically\.  

          The cash rewards can be easily withdrawn to any wallet of your choice\. This will be credited into your wallet's *credits* balance\.  
          *Note:* A particular player can be rewarded multiple times in different categories but not in the same category\. Do not create multiple accounts to spam the game, as we will know, and you will be suspended from the game and any reward rescinded\.  

          ***Leagues:***  
          Climb the ranks by earning more points per round and outperforming others in the leagues\.  

          ***Friends:***  
          Invite others to receive game bonuses\. ✨ More friends = more coins = more chances to win big prizes\! 🎁  

          ***The Purpose:***  
          Wordator is an addictive real-time multiplayer game where you accumulate points by participating in game rounds\.  
          Play as many rounds as you can—remember, you are competing against other players all around the world\. Show them what you've got\! Giving up is never an option\.  
          Other information is available within the app\.  

          Good luck!
